Land Cover One-Hot Encoding Scheme
==================================

A class is kept as its own column if it reaches 1.0% prevalence in EITHER file individually (not pooled across both).

landcover_local:
   2 (Evergreen_Broadleaf_Forest      ) SOURCE=0.40%                   -> other
   4 (Deciduous_Broadleaf_Forest      ) SOURCE=0.13%                   -> other
   8 (Woody_Savannas                  ) SOURCE=1.31%, PHILIPPINES=3.25% KEEP
   9 (Savannas                        ) SOURCE=9.30%, PHILIPPINES=10.11% KEEP
  10 (Grasslands                      ) SOURCE=2.74%                   KEEP
  12 (Croplands                       ) SOURCE=9.16%, PHILIPPINES=1.55% KEEP
  13 (Urban_and_Built_up              ) SOURCE=75.14%, PHILIPPINES=74.57% KEEP
  14 (Cropland_Natural_Vegetation_Mosaic) SOURCE=1.36%, PHILIPPINES=0.44% KEEP
  16 (Barren_or_Sparsely_Vegetated    ) SOURCE=0.11%                   -> other
  17 (Water                           ) SOURCE=0.33%, PHILIPPINES=10.08% KEEP
